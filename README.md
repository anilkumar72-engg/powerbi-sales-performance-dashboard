# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project focuses on building an executive-level Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI. The dashboard helps business leaders monitor sales growth, profitability, target achievement, product returns, delivery delays, and regional performance.

The objective was to transform raw operational data into a decision-making tool for monthly business reviews.

---

## Business Problem

The company operates across multiple regions and product categories. Leadership needed a dashboard that could answer:

* Is the business growing?
* Are sales targets being achieved?
* Which regions are performing best?
* Which categories are underperforming?
* Are returns increasing?
* Are delivery delays creating business risk?

---
